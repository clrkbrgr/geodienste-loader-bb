Services are loaded through the QGIS data source dialog, metadata opens in the Geoportal.
  Coverage: all services listed in the Geoportal Brandenburg, mainly from state authorities, districts and municipalities of Brandenburg. User interface in German and English.

  - Geodata providers of the Geoportal Brandenburg with their services,

  - Search across the entire catalogue.
